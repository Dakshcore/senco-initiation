## 2. Formalisation drivers
- BIS hallmarking became mandatory on 23 Jun 2021, district by district. The sixth phase (2 Mar 2026) added 7 districts, total 380. Mandatory carats: 14, 18, 20, 22, 23, 24. HUID is a six-digit alphanumeric code. IMPRI https://www.impriindia.com/insights/policy-update/bis-gold-hallmarking-2021-certifying-purity-and-building-consumer-trust-in-india/ ; All India Radio, 12 Mar 2026 https://www.newsonair.gov.in/govt-adds-7-more-districts-in-6th-phase-of-mandatory-gold-hallmarking
- HUID mandatory: sale without it prohibited after 31 Mar 2023. PIB title (snippet). https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=1904262&reg=48&lang=2
- 9-carat: AIR calls it voluntary; Senco's deck and IMPRI say mandatory from July 2025.
- PAN needed above Rs 2 lakh from Jan 2016; GST from Jul 2017. BOBCAPS report above, p.5.
- PMLA notification of 28 Dec 2020: KYC for jewellers' cash transactions of Rs 10 lakh or more (snippet, secondary). https://gjepc.org/solitaire/no-new-burden-of-compliance-on-gj-industry-under-pmla-rohan-shah/
- Gold Monetisation Scheme: medium and long-term deposits stopped from 26 Mar 2025 (snippet; the DEA page failed to load). https://dea.gov.in/schemes-services/gold-monetisation-scheme-2015
- Gold metal loan: Senco borrows at 3.4% p.a.; unfixed GML is 10-40% of inventory (Senco deck). Banks turned cautious on gold lending in Mar-Apr 2026 (Valorem Q1 FY27 call note). https://valoremadvisors.com/assets/admin/conference_file/1787725193_Senco_Gold_Ltd_Q1_FY27_Earnings_Concall_Highlights.pdf

## 6. Lab-grown diamonds and lightweight
- Senco: customers prefer lightweight, daily-wear, design-led pieces; wider 9K and 14K range; diamond jewellery up 43% in value and 18% in volume; gold volumes "broadly stable" (press release above).
- Senco's Sennes unit launched a lab-grown diamond business in 2025: 13 exclusive outlets, 3 multi-brand outlets. One large lab-grown-focused showroom opened in Q1 FY27.
- WGC (via Business Today, Jan 2026): buyers moved to lighter pieces; some younger northern urban buyers accepted 14-carat.
- Lab-grown export price per carat fell from USD 388.77 (2022) to USD 71.13 (Jan-May 2026); natural USD 865.50 to USD 757.91. Angel One, 14 Aug 2026. https://www.angelone.in/news/economy/india-s-lab-grown-diamond-exports-cross-natural-diamond-shipments-in-jan-may-2026
