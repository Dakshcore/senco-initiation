## Gold metal loans

GML has its own balance-sheet line ("Gold metal loans") under current financial liabilities, not under "Borrowings" and not in trade payables. It fell by 4,055.57 (cash flow statement, AFR p.9). The accounting policy treats bank gold taken on loan ("unfixed gold") as borrowings designated as a fair value hedge (AR p.295). I found no separate unfixed-gold payable inside trade payables.

## What drove the change

Other liabilities rose 11,320.71 (about Rs 1,132 cr). Three items explain 11,262: trade payables +5,269.05, other current liabilities +4,660.38, current tax +1,332.31 (less 265.52 in current other financial liabilities). Within other current liabilities, revenue received in advance added 3,845.69 and scheme deposits 504.99 (plus 261.24 now non-current). The IP groups these as "SY & Customer Advance": 8,484.1 vs 4,133.4 (IP p.10). The AR says the rise in contract liabilities is mainly cash received over revenue recognised (AR p.328). Trade payables rose alongside 61% inventory growth; acceptances (deferred payment arrangements) account for about 2,000 of it (AR p.326). Management said GML fell because the March gold spike forced positions to be squared off (TR p.15, printed p.14). My reading, not management's: payables and customer advances grew while GML shrank.
